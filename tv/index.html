How the alerts work

iPhone: tap Subscribe to calendar, then Settings › Apps › Calendar › Accounts › the subscription › keep Remove Alerts off. Each episode carries two alerts: one week out at 9 AM and the night before at 8 PM.

Android / Google Calendar: on the web, open Google Calendar › Other calendars › + › From URL, paste https://zactruelove.com/tv/schedule.ics. Google ignores alerts inside the feed, so open that calendar's settings and add two notifications: 1 week before and 1 day before (set the all-day version too). Google refreshes URL calendars roughly once a day.

The feed rebuilds every morning from TVmaze. Shows marked ignore or finished are left out of it.
